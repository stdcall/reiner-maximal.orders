# Maximal Orders

Irving Reiner. London Mathematical Society Monographs, New Series 28.
Clarendon Press, Oxford, 2003 (reissue of the edition of Academic Press,
London, 1975).

The book is an introduction to maximal orders in separable algebras over
Dedekind domains: orders over local and global rings, reduced norms and
discriminants, Morita equivalence, Brauer groups and crossed-product and
cyclic algebras, simple algebras over global fields, Eichler's Theorem,
class groups, Picard groups, hereditary orders and group rings.

The [releases](https://github.com/stdcall/reiner-maximal.orders/releases)
provide the book with editorial notes, a separate list of corrections and
SHA-256 checksums. The foreword of the 2003 reissue is not included.
